2. How Savify Works

Savify is built on a mission of transparency. Each subscription plan listed on the platform has a fixed number of seats and a fixed per-person split price, both shown to you before you pay. We operate under the following conditions:

  • Pay-first pools: You pay only your displayed share of the plan. Your seat in a pool is confirmed once your payment is successfully received.
  • Pool completion: A pool is complete when all of its seats have been paid for. Only then is the subscription arranged and access details issued to members.
  • No hidden charges: The amount displayed on the payment screen is the total amount payable. Any platform fee, if applicable, is shown separately before you confirm.
  • All payments for a pool share must be made through the Savify platform.
