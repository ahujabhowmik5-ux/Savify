1. Overview

This policy outlines the terms for cancellations and refunds of the amount you pay to join a subscription pool on Savify. Our goal is to provide a clear process when a pool does not proceed as planned. This policy is part of our main Terms & Conditions.

2. Scope of this Policy

This Refund Policy applies to the per-person share you pay to Savify to reserve a seat in a pool. It does not apply to:

  • Any amount you pay directly to a subscription provider outside the Savify platform.
  • Any private arrangement made between members of a pool outside the platform.

3. When You Are Entitled to a Full Refund

Because Savify is pay-first, your money sits with us until your pool is complete. You are entitled to a full refund of the amount you paid in the following cases:

  • The pool does not fill: If the remaining seats in your pool are not taken, you may request a refund at any time and your payment is returned to you in full. Your money stays with Savify until the pool completes — it is never released to anyone else while seats remain open.
  • We cannot deliver access: If we are unable to provide the subscription access for your completed pool within 48 hours of completion, your payment is refunded in full.
  • Duplicate or failed payment: If you were charged more than once for the same seat, or an amount was debited without a seat being allocated, the extra amount is refunded in full.
  • Cancellation before completion: If you ask to leave a pool before it is complete and before access details have been issued, your payment is refunded in full.

4. When a Refund Is Not Available

  • After access has been delivered: Once your pool is complete and the subscription access details have been issued to you, the service has been delivered and the payment is non-refundable.
  • Breach of terms: If access issued to you is shared, resold, or published outside your pool in breach of our Terms & Conditions.
  • Third-party changes: Where a subscription provider changes its own plan terms, pricing, or sharing rules after your access has been delivered.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please email savifyhq@gmail.com with the subject "Refund: [Your Plan Name]". You must include:

  • The plan or pool name and the date of payment.
  • The registered email address or phone number on your Savify account.
  • The payment or order reference, and a payment screenshot if available.
  • A clear explanation of the issue.

6. Refund Timelines

  • We acknowledge every refund request within 2 business days.
  • Approved refunds are initiated within 5 business days of approval.
  • Refunds are credited to your original payment method, and typically appear within 5 to 7 business days of being initiated, depending on your bank or payment provider.
  • Refunds are always issued to the original payment method used for the order. We do not refund to a different account.

7. Cancellation

You may cancel your participation in a pool at any time before that pool is complete and before access details have been issued, by contacting us at savifyhq@gmail.com or on +91 8517801653. Cancellation after delivery is not possible, as the service has already been provided.
